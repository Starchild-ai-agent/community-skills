## Why CHIP wins
- On-chain anchors (DeFiLlama) vs protocol-claimed ($500M vs $311.8M) explicitly separated; paper anchors to the lower verified figure.
- Recomputed true float 4.13% vs marketing's 15% — corrects the source material.
- Pipeline table separates EXECUTED / LIVE / TERM SHEET / PASSED GOV.
- MM deal analysis is adversarial (Selini 2% token loan = free call option; Third Eye cleaner).
- Risks quantified with magnitudes (April $5.7M wave vs $12.4M float; October S2 step-up 5.43%→8.13%).

## Why Monad loses
- Core tokenomics numbers (49% circ, 11.2% float, 3.3% airdrop) have NO source attribution.
- Central sell-through assumption (55-80% airdrop, HYPE/ENA/JUP/XPL precedents) asserted, methodology not shown.
- Survey of n=13 framed as "expectations of market participants."
- Valuation = straight FDV comps + midpoint, no float-adjusted view in the 09/25 note; $2bn floor (2023) vs $4.7bn anchor (2025) unreconciled.
- Heavy OCR typos throughout (no QC pass).
